The story: The Harmony Run

All fifteen worlds of this galaxy once sang one song of light called the Harmony. Then a crackling cloud called the Static crept out of the galactic core and jammed it. Aliens who were friends now hear threats in every word. You are a Wayfinder. Your blaster fires Harmony pulses that shake the Static loose. Free the worlds, earn the Harmony Shards, and find out where the Static really comes from.

How to play

On a computer

Drag to spin the camera all the way around and scroll to zoom. W, A, S and D move. Space jumps, and holding Space in the air fires your jetpack. Click or press F to fire, E to talk, Q to swap weapons and H to holster your blaster.

On a phone or tablet

Drag to spin, pinch to zoom, and use the thumb joystick to move. The big buttons fire, jump, jet, talk and swap weapons.

In a VR headset

Open thevrgalaxy.com in your headset browser, such as the Meta Quest Browser, and tap Enter VR. The left stick moves, the right stick turns, the trigger fires and the grip is your jetpack.

The worlds

Two worlds are open from the start and ten more unlock as you earn Harmony Shards. Three more are hidden, and the only way to find them is to play, collect Star Glyphs and follow the clues.

  • LumoraA jungle that glows brighter when you laugh. Open from the start.
  • GloopaBouncy, gooey, and a little bit sticky. Open from the start.
  • Vertigo PeaksMountains so tall they scrape the stars. Unlocks at 1 Harmony Shard.
  • Neon RiftA city that never sleeps and never stops glowing. Unlocks at 2 Harmony Shards.
  • Coaster NebulaThe galaxy’s wildest ride, loops included. Unlocks at 3 Harmony Shards.
  • CrystalisCrystal caves that sing when you touch them. Unlocks at 4 Harmony Shards.
  • MagmaraLava rivers, fire trees, and a very hot temper. Unlocks at 5 Harmony Shards.
  • AquaraAn ocean world where islands float on songs. Unlocks at 6 Harmony Shards.
  • MechanicaA robot factory that forgot what it was building. Unlocks at 7 Harmony Shards.
  • SporeveilGiant mushrooms, glowing spores, sleepy giants. Unlocks at 8 Harmony Shards.
  • CloudriftIslands of candy-colored sky. Unlocks at 9 Harmony Shards.
  • DunestarTwin suns, singing dunes, and a buried secret. Unlocks at 10 Harmony Shards.

Weapons, friends and Orbi

Every weapon fires Harmony, not harm: the Tuner Blaster, Slime Launcher, Ring Cannon, Prism Beam and Gravity Orb. Robot drones pop apart, while jammed aliens get freed and become friends who share secrets. Orbi, your floating guide bot, explains the controls, tells jokes, projects holograms and drops careful clues. Pick an astronaut, or upload a selfie and wear your own face on your explorer.

Questions

Is theVRgalaxy free to play?

Yes. theVRgalaxy is a free VR game that runs in your web browser. There is nothing to download or install and no account to create.

Do I need a VR headset to play?

No. The game runs in 3D on a desktop, laptop, phone or tablet. If you have a headset, open the site in its browser and tap Enter VR to play in full virtual reality through WebXR.

How do I play on a Meta Quest?

Open thevrgalaxy.com in the Meta Quest Browser and tap Enter VR. The left stick moves, the right stick turns, the trigger fires and the grip button fires your jetpack.

How many worlds are in theVRgalaxy?

There are 15 worlds. Two are open from the start, ten unlock as you earn Harmony Shards, and three are hidden. The hidden worlds are only found by playing, collecting Star Glyphs and following clues.

Is the game violent?

No blood and no gore. Your weapons fire Harmony pulses that free jammed aliens and turn them into friends. Only robot Static drones pop apart, and the story ends with everyone getting along.

Does the game save my progress?

Yes. Your shards, glyphs, friends, weapons and character are saved automatically in your browser on the device you play on.
