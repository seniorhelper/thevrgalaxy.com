The 15 worlds of theVRgalaxy

theVRgalaxy is a free VR game in your browser with fifteen worlds to explore. Each one is huge, has its own sky, creatures and mission, and hides five Star Glyphs. Here are the twelve worlds everyone can find.

Two worlds are open from the start. Every Harmony Shard you earn wakes up another one. The last three worlds are hidden. They are not listed here on purpose: you find them by playing, collecting glyphs and following clues from Orbi and the aliens you free.

World 1

Lumora

A jungle that glows brighter when you laugh.

Mission: Orbi dropped his Spark Cells. Find all 6.

Open from the start.
